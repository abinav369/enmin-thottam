It is my goal to write this entire site exclusively in Thamizh. And when I say Thamizh, I mean pure, 
classical Thamizh—unmixed with **English, Prakrit or Sanskrit**. Therefore, if you, as a reader, is Thamizh, 
<Pu message="If you are already here reading in English and you know Thamizh please switch to thamizh. You are Thamizh

translations and read the Thamizh text itself.
